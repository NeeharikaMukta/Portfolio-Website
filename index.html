About Me

I'm Neeharika, an Oracle APEX developer who enjoys turning messy business processes into clean, well-structured database systems. My work spans data modeling, PL/SQL development and building Oracle APEX applications that people actually want to use — for supply chain, sales and inventory-heavy workflows.

My background is in Chemistry, and that analytical training carries straight into how I approach data: break the problem down, understand the structure, and build something that holds up under real use. I later trained through the IsDB-BISEW IT Scholarship Programme in Oracle Database Application Development, which is where I built the technical foundation I use every day now.

Outside of client work, I like exploring new corners of the Oracle ecosystem and sharpening my skills in cloud-based database environments.
